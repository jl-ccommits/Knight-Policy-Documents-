## One door: brokerage@carma365.com

1. **Everything brokerage goes in and out through brokerage@:** submissions, quotes, binds, subjectivities, endorsements, renewals, carrier notices, and agent questions about premium or commission. Replies go from brokerage@, never from a personal inbox or service@.
2. **Whoever gets brokerage work anywhere else forwards it to brokerage@ the same day**, with the handoff note in Appendix A. That covers Summer, Carma underwriting (Zimraan, Tejas), Bryan, and anyone reading service@, sales@, premium@ or ap@. Once it is in brokerage@, the desk owns it. No work is assigned by Teams DM or text; the Brokerage X Service chat is for quick internal questions only.
3. **Every carrier, wholesaler and portal points to brokerage@** for notices, billing and login verification codes. No portal login depends on one person's inbox.
4. **The inbox shows its own state.** At triage, every email in the Inbox gets one Outlook category: `MATT` or `KHELU`. When the action is done and the email is saved to C360, it moves to the `Done` folder. No category means nobody has picked it up. Still in the Inbox after 1 business day means late. Waiting on an agent or carrier is a C360 task, not a reason to leave the email in the Inbox.
5. **Accounting copies brokerage@.** Amanda and Maia copy brokerage@ on any premium@ or ap@ email about a brokerage file.
6. **Documents** go to the C360 account and the Named Insured folder, named as in Appendix A. Nothing is saved to a desktop.

**Access:** Matthew and Kheluram need Full Access and Send As on brokerage@, and edit rights to C360 brokerage functions. Summer and Jacob need Full Access and C360 view. Jacob updates access in the same week anyone joins, leaves or changes role.

## The critical path

Every file, new business or renewal, goes through these eight steps. Matthew selects and releases quotes. The clock starts when the item lands in brokerage@.

Each step has one owner: the person who answers for the deadline, whoever does the work. When a step is done, the owner closes its C360 task and opens the next one.

| # | Step | Owner | Deadline | Done when | C360 stage |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Log | Kheluram | Same business day | Agent told it's received. C360 account created. State checked in the SLA Clearance Tool. | New Business |
| 2 | Complete | Kheluram | Same business day | One email to the agent lists everything missing (app, loss runs, SOV, supplementals, license). Nothing goes to market incomplete. | New Business |
| 3 | Market | Matthew | 1 business day after the file is complete | Matthew picks markets from the Marketing Matrix and Submission Routing Playbook. Kheluram sends from brokerage@ or the portal, per Hard rules 1 and 2. | Follow Up |
| 4 | Log quote | Kheluram | Same day it arrives | Every quote and decline saved to C360 with its premium | Quote |
| 5 | Select | Matthew | Same day as step 4 | C360 note: which quote and why, and who confirmed cannabis and when. Lead with the lowest total cost after Carma taxes and fees, among quotes that match what the agent asked for. If two are close or trade coverage for price, send both with a Marketing Summary. | Quote |
| 6 | Propose | Matthew | 1 business day after the quote arrives | Kheluram builds it: the ten changes, cover letter and T&F figures. Matthew runs the Final Quality Checklist and releases it from brokerage@. | Quote |
| 7 | Bind | Matthew | Same business day as the bind request | Bind confirmed to the agent. Bind order to the carrier with signed quote forms, signed application, Diligent Effort form and payment. | Closed |
| 8 | Close out | Matthew | Binder and invoice to agent: 1 business day after the binder arrives. Subjectivities to carrier: 1 business day after they arrive. Premium: per Money at bind. Policy: checked against the quote, SL filed and delivered within 2 business days. | Each item is its own C360 task until done. Folder moved to .BOUND BUSINESS. | Closed |

**Kheluram owns the chasing**, each chase as its own task: any market silent for 3 business days (step 3), and the agent every 3 business days after a proposal until it is bound, declined or expired (step 6).

**Renewals** run the same steps on a fixed clock, whether or not the agent asks:
- 90 days out, Kheluram does steps 1 and 2. He asks for the current application, ACORDs, currently valued loss runs and a signed loss-run authorization, with a summary of current coverage.
- 60 days out, Matthew does step 3.
- 30 days out, Matthew does step 6.
- 15 days out with no bind request, Matthew calls the agent and tells Summer.

**Service requests** (endorsements, cancellations, audits, loss runs): Kheluram acknowledges the same day and sends to the carrier within 1 business day. The task stays open until the agent has the carrier's answer.

**Same-day interrupts** go ahead of everything else:
- Carrier warning: see Carrier warnings and money.
- Bind request: step 7.
- An agent asks about the same item a second time: Matthew calls that day instead of emailing.
- Effective date within 5 business days and not bound: Matthew works the file himself.

**Cover is automatic.** Anything that arrives after Kheluram's shift, or while he is out, is Matthew's. When Matthew is out, Kheluram takes his tasks. Summer handles bind requests, carrier warnings and proposal release. Before a planned absence, the person going out reassigns their open C360 tasks.

Summer signs off these deadlines by Sep 25, 2026. After that, this is the only place deadlines are set.

## Statuses: one stage, one task

C360 is the only place that records where a file stands. Managers read C360. Nobody keeps or hand-builds a second tracker.

A file's status is two things in C360.

**1. Its stage**, one of the five C360 pipeline stages:

| Stage | The file is here when |
| --- | --- |
| New Business | Received, being cleared, or waiting on the agent for missing items. Renewals enter here at 90 days. |
| Follow Up | At markets, waiting on quotes |
| Quote | At least one quote is in. The proposal is being built, or it is sent and waiting on a bind request. |
| Closed | Bound. Stays on the daily list while any close-out task is open. |
| Declined | Declined, lost or not placed. No open task. |

**2. An open task for each next action**, assigned to Matthew or Kheluram and due on the critical-path deadline. Name it `[step #] [step name] – [Named Insured]`, for example `6 Propose – Ethos` or `8 Subjectivities – Mederi Express`. Service requests and agent money questions use `S [topic] – [Named Insured]`. Carrier warnings use `! Warning – [Named Insured]`. Close a task and open the next one in the same sitting. Anything with its own due date gets its own task: each subjectivity, premium, a chase or a warning.

**Late means an overdue task. Unowned means an open file with no task.** A manager only needs those two C360 views. Never move a due date to hide a miss; write the new date in the task note.

There is no separate status column, follow-up date or proposal-pending status. A Quote-stage file with an open `6 Propose` task is a proposal pending.

**The NB Log is history, not a tracker.** It goes read-only once C360's brokerage errors are fixed and the task views work (Go-live checklist). Until then, keep it current as the backup record; if the two disagree, C360 wins. After cutover, no more dated copies are saved.

**When C360 is down**, new items go on one sheet with a fixed name: `Brokerage > Shared Documents > C360 Downtime Log.xlsx`. It has seven columns: Date, Named Insured, Agent, What came in, Next action, Owner, Due. Never save a dated copy. Kheluram back-fills C360 the day it recovers and clears the sheet.

## Carrier warnings and money

Both stay open until someone outside the desk confirms in writing. A reply or a request does not close them.

**Carrier warnings.** A carrier warning is any cancellation, non-pay, payment-draft, nonrenewal, audit or reinstatement notice. It is also any notice that Carma's agency or appointment is suspended. The day one arrives, Matthew:

1. Replies to the carrier, calls the agent and opens `! Warning – [Named Insured]`, due on the carrier's deadline.
2. Sends any agency or appointment notice to Summer, who fixes the appointment. Matthew still owns the task.
3. Closes the task only when the carrier confirms in writing that the issue is resolved, and that confirmation is saved to C360.

A second warning on the same policy goes to Jacob the same day.

**Money at bind.** Matthew owns every line. Accounting makes the payments.

1. **Billing type comes from the binder.** Copy `Agency bill` or `Direct bill` from the carrier binder into the C360 note and the binder email to the agent. Never state it from memory.
2. **Agency bill.** Invoice the agent net of commission, with remittance by policy. At bind, open `8 Premium – [Named Insured]`, due 3 business days before the carrier's due date. The day the agent pays, email premium@, cc brokerage@, subject `PAY CARRIER – [Named Insured] – [Carrier] – $[amount] – due [MM/DD]`. Close the task when Maia Root replies on that thread with the date paid.
3. **Direct bill.** The carrier bills the insured. Email ap@, subject `COMMISSION – [Named Insured] – [Carrier] – $[amount]`, so Amanda Ingersol enters it in Advance. The binder email tells the agent how and when commission is paid.
4. **Agent money questions stay with Matthew.** Forward the question to premium@ or ap@, open `S Commission – [Named Insured]` or `S Premium – [Named Insured]` due in 2 business days, and give the agent the answer yourself. Accounting helps but never owns it.

## Hard rules

No exceptions, and no approval needed to say no. Deadlines are in the critical path. Approvals are in What Summer approves. Money is in Carrier warnings and money.

1. **Cannabis disclosed.** Every submission email and portal note for a cannabis business opens with: "Cannabis risk: [operation type], licensed in [state]. Please quote on that basis and confirm it in your quote." No quote goes to an agent until the market has confirmed cannabis in writing. The locations and limits must also match the SOV.
2. **Retail agent stays private.** No carrier, wholesaler or MGA gets the retail agent's name or contact details. If a market asks, the retail agent is Bryan Mosser.
3. **Licensed states only.** No E&S in a state the SLA Clearance Tool flags.
4. **Clean retail proposals.** No carrier contact details, carrier tax lines or total commission, and no admin fee below the band minimum.
5. **Coverage changes only in writing.** Add or change coverage only on the client's written instruction. Then confirm the carrier issued the endorsement and send it to the client.

## What Summer approves, and escalation

Summer approves only the items below. Everything else Matthew decides and releases himself. To ask, Matthew emails brokerage@, subject `SUMMER APPROVAL – [Named Insured]`, with his recommendation. Summer answers the same business day.

- Premium of $40,000 or more. The admin fee is 3% of premium and needs management approval before the proposal goes out.
- A market that is not in the Marketing Matrix, or needs a new appointment (for example, Earth Quality Products needs a Kinsale appointment).
- Leading with a quote that gives up coverage the agent asked for, such as property excluded.
- The first two files in any line the team hasn't written before. Flood for Ethos is the current one.
- Declining an account Summer brought in, or telling an agent Carma can't place it.
- Any agent complaint or relationship issue.

**Escalation**

1. If a critical-path deadline will be missed, it goes on line 3 of that day's EOD email, with the owner and new date, before the deadline passes.
2. If Summer hasn't answered an approval by end of day, Matthew escalates to Jacob.
3. A system problem (C360 error, mailbox access, portal login) goes to Jacob the same day. While C360 is down, work goes on the C360 Downtime Log.

**Summer's after-the-fact review:** in weeks 1–2, 15 minutes a day on every proposal released that day, with feedback in writing. In weeks 3–4, 3 files a week. From week 5, approvals and the Monday review only.

## Daily and weekly rhythm

**Triage order**, for Matthew at the start of the day and Kheluram at the start of his shift: first same-day interrupts (see the critical path), then overdue tasks, oldest first, then tasks due today, then new brokerage@ email.

**End-of-day email.** Matthew sends it from brokerage@ to Summer, cc Jacob, with the subject `EOD – [MM/DD]`. It has the same five lines every day, counted from C360 and the brokerage Inbox:

1. Done today: [#] bound, [#] proposals sent, with Named Insureds
2. Carrier warnings open: [#], each with the carrier's deadline
3. Late or will be late: [#], each with owner and new date (target 0)
4. Open files with no task, plus Inbox emails with no owner category: [#] (target 0)
5. Needs Summer: [items], or "none"

Anything this SOP says to tell Summer goes on line 5 that day. If Matthew is out, Kheluram sends the email.

**Monday review**, 30 minutes, with Matthew, Kheluram, Summer and Jacob. Cover lines 2 to 4 and last week's totals for all five lines. Every late file leaves the meeting with one owner and a date. Summer names any file she had to work herself that week (target 0). The full pipeline is not walked.

## Appendix A: Templates

**Handoff note** (anyone forwarding brokerage work to brokerage@)

One short email per new account, subject `NEW BIZ – [Named Insured] – [LOB] – Eff [MM/DD/YY]`:

- Agent and agency, with email
- Lines of business and effective date
- Incumbent carrier and premium, if known
- Anything promised to the agent (markets, price, timing)
- Attachments: application, loss runs, SOV, supplementals

**File names** (match what is already in C360):

- `26-27 [LOB] - Submission Email to [Carrier]`
- `26-27 [LOB] [Carrier] QUOTE $[premium]`
- `26-27 [LOB] - Proposal to Agent`
- `26-27 [LOB] - Bind Request` / `Binder` / `Policy`

**Refunds** show the transaction type and a negative amount.
